# Support

When requesting support, provide app release tags, the Frappe/ERPNext/HRMS v16
commit set, site health output, exact steps and a sanitised traceback. Do not send
production personal or statutory data through public channels.

Legal interpretation, SARS/Department portal acceptance, bank acceptance and
company policy approval require the appropriate independent practitioner or
institution; software support cannot replace those approvals.

External SARS, Department of Employment and Labour, Compensation Fund, CIPC and
bank actions are controlled/manual unless the relevant released guide explicitly
documents a tested integration and its acceptance evidence.
