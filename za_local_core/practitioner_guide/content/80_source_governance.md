# Statutory source governance

Every statutory value must be effective-dated and traceable to an approved source. Record the authority, title, version, publication and effective dates, source URL or private attachment, checksum, reviewer and impacted capabilities.

For 2026/27 the payroll baseline was checked against the SARS 2027 Employer Guide and 2026 Budget Tax Guide. The COIDA R668,000 ceiling is from General Notice 3910 of 2026, Government Gazette 54577, effective 1 March 2026.

Resolve historical documents by their payroll or transaction date—not the current date. Missing or overlapping approved configuration must block calculation. Annual and mid-year changes require a change review, regression tests on both sides of the effective date, and practitioner approval.
