# South African workspaces

- SA Compliance: source approvals, readiness, filings, receipts, POPIA and PAIA.
- SA VAT: VAT settings, transactions, VAT201 working papers and tax-document reports.
- SA Payroll: employees, payroll runs, declarations, certificates and payment batches.
- SA Labour / SA COIDA: skills and EE working papers, injuries, claims and annual assessment.

Available links depend on your role and company permissions. If sensitive information is visible outside your job scope, stop and report the access issue to the System Manager or Information Officer.
