# Exporting and printing

Preview statutory and commercial documents before sharing them. Confirm the company, period, registration details,
totals and document status. A PDF, CSV or spreadsheet is a working paper/export unless the applicable released
guide documents a tested authority or bank format and retained acceptance evidence. Feature Readiness alone does
not turn an export into an accepted submission.

Export only the minimum information required for an authorised purpose. Use private attachments and approved secure transfer channels for payroll, banking, tax, demographic, injury or medical information.
